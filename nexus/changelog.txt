v1.0.2 - 2026-09-10
Added: A smarter check reads the game's own loot signal, so decor copies of loot props and used-up containers stay dark while real loot still glows.
Fixed: Some objects glowed in focus mode but never showed a loot prompt, such as lighting towers and trash cans placed as unreachable base decor. These no longer glow, while objects you can loot still do.
Fixed: A battery antidote dispenser stops glowing once its antidote is taken.
Fixed: Objects placed outside the playable map no longer glow.

v1.0.1 - 2026-09-05
Added: Fuel cans now glow in focus mode, red by default to match the game's own explosive-can highlight. The color and an on/off toggle are in the config.
Fixed: Cleaner rendering of highlighted objects - no more stray spikes, bright squares, or outlines on nearby foliage.
Fixed: Some unreachable decorations, such as trash piles on military bases, are no longer highlighted.
Fixed: Mobile lighting towers are highlighted again; they can hold loot depending on placement.
